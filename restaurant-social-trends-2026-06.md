# Restaurant Social Media Trends Report — June 2026

**Prepared:** June 16, 2026 · **Scope:** US-focused, last ~30–60 days · **For:** Plush Marketing Agency

This is a working playbook of what's trending **right now** across TikTok and Instagram, plus the **timely events** worth riding this summer — each paired with a **practical "how a restaurant uses it"** angle. Click any link to view the source video, creator, or article.

**How to read the links:** Where a real example video exists, it's linked directly. TikTok hides many individual video URLs from search, so some links go to the trend's TikTok "Discover" hub (a live feed of that trend) or to a reputable article that documents and examples it. Recency is flagged as 🟢 Rising, 🟡 Peaking, or 🔴 Fading so you know what to chase vs. skip.

## 1) General Viral Trends (TikTok & Instagram Reels)

### Trending Audios / Sounds

**Charli XCX — "Rock Music" stuck-frame edit** 🟢🟡
A deliberate vocal glitch mid-song; creators sync a clip to the glitch then freeze ("stuck frame") on the best visual. One of the biggest sounds of June 2026.
🔗 [Trend explainer (Yahoo)](https://www.yahoo.com/news/videos/tiktok-thursday-were-trying-charli-175200377.html) · [Song info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_Music_(Charli_XCX_song))
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** Film prep b-roll, then freeze on the cheese pull / the sear / the finished plate landing on the table. Perfect for a new-item reveal.

**Temper City — "Self Aware" (cinematic reflection sound)** 🟢
Dreamy, reflective montages (golden-hour b-roll, slow walks) with text overlays. 434K+ videos.
🔗 [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/rock/temper-city-self-aware-june-2026-chartbreaker-1236268696/)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** A "morning open" montage — unlocking the door, grinding coffee, first loaf out of the oven — with a line about the craft behind your food.

**Olivia Rodrigo — "Drop Dead" ("the most alive I've ever been")** 🟢🟡
Emotional lyric-overlay format over milestones and "core memories." Song hit No. 1.
🔗 [Example video](https://www.tiktok.com/@livbedumb/video/7629579050872294669) · [Context](https://stevenknightmedia.com/olivia-rodrigo-s-drop-dead-hits-no.-1-reviving-the-cure)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** Anniversary/opening-day throwback, or a regular's "core memory" booth/dish montage.

**Tavares — "Heaven Must Be Missing an Angel" (Reels summer anthem)** 🟢
Nostalgic disco over "perfect moment" and summer-recap clips.
🔗 [Reels audio page](https://www.instagram.com/reels/audio/185706688978501/heaven-must-be-missing-an-angel/)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** Patio/rooftop summer-vibe recap; "the perfect summer plate" reveal.

### Meme Formats

**"6-7" (six-seven) / "Doot Doot"** 🟡🔴
A meaningless slang interjection + finger-counting gesture; saturated but everywhere. Dictionary.com 2025 Word of the Year.
🔗 [Explainer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-7_(meme)) · [Example](https://www.tiktok.com/@knowyourmeme/video/7522198976020466957)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** Light, youth-skewing only — e.g. a "6-7" bit on a $6.70 special. Use sparingly; it reads as Gen Alpha/ironic.

**Italian Brainrot (AI animals: Bombardiro Crocodilo, Tralalero Tralala)** 🟡
Surreal AI creature mashups with Italian TTS narration. Endlessly remixable.
🔗 [Guide](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-are-the-italian-brainrot-animals-the-ai-memes-like-bombardiro-crocodilo-taking-over-tiktok-explained)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** Italian spots especially — an AI "mascot" character to introduce specials. Niche/playful brands only.

**"Bad news for my wallet…" deadpan text overlay** 🟢
One photo + deadpan caption about an obsession you keep spending on. One version hit 12.1M views.
🔗 [Breakdown](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/bad-news-for-my-wallet-i-woke-up-12-1m-views) · [Reel example](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYbn4KGMZB3/)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** "Bad news for my wallet, the [signature dish] is back on the menu." Photo of the dish, done.

**"I'm also just a girl" (Notting Hill quote)** 🟡
The Julia Roberts line over girly/aesthetic clips.
🔗 [Trend hub](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/notting-hill-im-just-a-girl)
🍽️ **Restaurant use:** "I'm just a girl… who needs a matcha and a croissant." Pair with your most photogenic order.

## 2) Viral Food & Drink Trends

**Dubai Chocolate — Strawberry Cup evolution** 🟡 (base bar 🔴)
Pistachio cream + crispy kataifi + chocolate, now layered with fresh strawberries. The ASMR "crack" is the whole point.
🔗 [Example video](https://www.tiktok.com/@kyanierocap/video/7455179659345513774) · [Trend](https://tastewise.io/blog/dubai-chocolate-trend)
🍽️ **Use:** A dessert special or LTO; film the crack/pull in close-up ASMR.

**"Swicy" (sweet + spicy)** 🟡
Hot honey on everything, mango-habanero, chili-dusted sweets. +49% sweet-and-spicy launches in 2026.
🔗 [Trend](https://tastewise.io/blog/swicy-food-trends)
🍽️ **Use:** Hot-honey drizzle LTO, swicy wings, or a chili-mango dessert. Cheapest on-trend menu move available.

**"Swalty" (sweet + salty)** 🟢
Salted caramel, miso cookies, honey + sea salt. Projected ~32% category growth by 2028.
🔗 [Trend](https://www.specialityfoodmagazine.com/food-and-drink/swalty-food-trend-sweet-salty)
🍽️ **Use:** Salted-caramel or miso dessert; a salted-honey latte.

**Starbucks Blue Coconut Refresher & Blue Coconut Matcha** 🟢 (launched June 16)
Striking blue (blue spirulina) summer drinks tied to a clean-water campaign.
🔗 [Starbucks newsroom](https://about.starbucks.com/stories/2026/starbucks-introduces-blue-coconut-duo-supporting-clean-water-through-get-blue/) · [Parade](https://parade.com/food/starbucks-new-blue-matcha-refresher-flavors)
🍽️ **Use:** A naturally-blue signature drink is instantly shareable. Cafés/bars: ride the color.

**Coconut iced coffee & flavored matcha** 🟡 (watch the backlash)
Coconut iced coffee + endless matcha flavors (lavender, mango, horchata). Matcha menu items +30% YoY — but a "matcha backlash" is emerging.
🔗 [Summer coffee trends](https://foodiesonly.in/blogs/summer-coffee-trends-2026) · [Backlash](https://creators.yahoo.com/lifestyle/story/everyone-is-ordering-matcha-in-2026-so-why-are-so-many-people-turning-on-it-034839922.html)
🍽️ **Use:** A flavored-matcha LTO; coconut cold brew for the tropical look.

**Dirty Soda / Protein Soda** 🟢
Soda built up with cream + syrups; 2026 twist is adding protein ("protein Diet Coke").
🔗 [Protein Diet Coke](https://www.foxnews.com/food-drink/viral-protein-diet-coke-trend-sweeps-social-media-users-surprised-good-drink) · [Dirty Mountain Dew](https://www.foodnetwork.com/fn-dish/news/mountain-dew-dirty-soda-cream)
🍽️ **Use:** A "dirty soda" bar / build-your-own menu; great UGC driver.

**Strawberry Cheesecake Dip** 🟢
4-ingredient no-bake dip dubbed "the unofficial dip of summer 2026."
🔗 [Example](https://www.tiktok.com/@recipes/video/7315103492564159752)
🍽️ **Use:** Shareable dessert app; post the easy "recipe reveal."

**Cottage cheese everything (high-protein)** 🟡
Cottage-cheese cucumber salads, tacos, ice cream. So popular it caused grocery shortages.
🔗 [Trend roundup](https://canadiangrocer.com/most-viral-tiktok-food-trends-2026-so-far)
🍽️ **Use:** A high-protein menu callout; health-skewing spots especially.

**Japanese strawberries & Ichigo (fruit) sando** 🟢
Premium Japanese strawberries in layered cakes; the strawberry-cream sando. ~28% YoY growth.
🔗 [The Week](https://theweek.com/culture-life/food-drink/japanese-style-sandos-a-smash-hit) · [Trend](https://tastewise.io/blog/viral-food-trends)

Tinned fish styled like charcuterie; photogenic and sustainable.
🔗 [Example](https://www.tiktok.com/@wickdconfections/video/7541993708925832478) · [Trend](https://tastewise.io/blog/tinned-fish-trend)

## The two playbooks to anchor everything on

1. **The Chili's "Triple Dipper" model** — engineer one dish to be *inherently filmable* (the cheese pull), then **amplify customer videos** instead of buying ads. Triple Dipper sales rose ~70% YoY off this. 🔗 [Case study](https://www.nrn.com/casual-dining/chili-s-turns-viral-triple-dipper-trend-into-a-new-social-media-marketing-push)
2. **The Crumbl "weekly drop" model** — a **scheduled reveal** (same time weekly) manufactures a recurring UGC engine of taste-tests and rankings. 🔗 [How it works](https://influencity.com/blog/en/crumbl-cookies-product-launch-strategy-broke-tiktok)

**Execution basics:** 2–3 posts/week minimum; hook in the first 3 seconds; ride trending audio fast (trends move in days); define 4–6 content "buckets" so there's always something to post; repost customer UGC to build a flywheel; favor **micro-influencers / UGC** over pricey macro-influencer asks (the backlash against $1,200–$1,800 "collab" fees is real). 🔗 [Restaurant TikTok guide](https://www.malou.io/en-us/blog/tiktok-for-restaurants) · [Influencer-cost backlash](https://www.dexerto.com/food/restaurant-owner-goes-viral-after-calling-out-influencer-asking-for-1800-to-eat-there-3373379/)
